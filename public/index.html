This clears your tasks, daily checkpoints, calendar history, streaks and target date. Your account and sign-in method will remain unchanged. This cannot be undone.

Your Daymark account and tracker document will be deleted. You will be signed out and will not be able to recover this data. You may need to verify your identity again before deletion.
